iMail Secure is an email client for iOS that puts privacy first: full IMAP/SMTP support, read receipts per RFC 8098 – and no tracking, no ads, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs.

PRIVACY BY DEFAULT
• Emails are shown as text by default. HTML is rendered only when you tap the button – without network access and without JavaScript.
• Remote images and stylesheets are blocked. Allow them for a single email or permanently for one sender – revocable at any time.
• Tracking parameters are stripped from links before you open them.
• Passwords are stored only in the iOS keychain (ThisDeviceOnly).
• Encrypted connections only: TLS 1.2 or newer, with certificate pinning.
• No account with us, no cloud: your mail stays with your provider and on your device.

FEATURES
• IMAP and SMTP for all common providers (GMX, Web.de, Outlook, Gmail, T-Online, your own server)
• Every folder on the server – including Trash, Sent, Drafts, Archive and Spam
• Request and answer read receipts (MDN) and delivery receipts
• Save and share attachments safely
• Search subject, sender and body
• Multiple accounts and a unified inbox (Pro)

EVERYTHING THAT PROTECTS YOU STAYS FREE
The free version includes all privacy features and one account. If you want more convenience – additional accounts, your own folders, a unified inbox – you can subscribe to iMail Secure Pro. The first week is free, then €1.99 per month or €17.99 per year.

Privacy features will never be paid.
