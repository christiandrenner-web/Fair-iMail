iMail Secure requires a real IMAP/SMTP mailbox; there is no demo mode. Please use the test account below (credentials in demo_user.txt / demo_password.txt). The account uses standard ports (IMAP 993 implicit TLS, SMTP 587 STARTTLS). No login, purchase or account creation is required to open the app. Subscriptions are StoreKit auto-renewable subscriptions in the group "iMail Secure Pro"; the 7-day free trial is an introductory offer configured in App Store Connect.
